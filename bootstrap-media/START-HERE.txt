DOTFILES BOOTSTRAP
==================

Kubuntu: open a terminal in this drive and run:
  bash START-LINUX.sh

WSL: open the USB drive through /mnt/<drive-letter> and run:
  bash START-LINUX.sh

Windows: double-click START-WINDOWS.cmd.

The drive contains no passwords, tokens, private repositories, or vault export.
Internet access and interactive administrator, GitHub, Bitwarden, and 2FA prompts
are required.
